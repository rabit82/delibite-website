About DeliBite

DeliBite is a fresh salad brand created for people who want clean, healthy, and affordable food.

The idea came from the need for ready-to-eat salads that feel safe, fresh, and easy to order.

Our goal is to make healthy eating simple for busy students, workers, families, and busy moms.
